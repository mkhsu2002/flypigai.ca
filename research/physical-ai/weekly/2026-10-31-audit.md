- Prediction effects: FP002 weakens weak; FP003 weakens weak; FP001 neutral weak.
- Primary: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/adjusting-imports-of-robotics-industrial-machinery-and-their-parts-and-components-into-the-united-states/
