# FlyPig AI repository instructions

Read `README.md` and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-25-site-stabilization-design.md` before changing the site.

General deployment, backend-contract, secret-handling, browser-lane, and release rules come from the parent/global instructions. This file contains only FlyPig AI-specific requirements.

## Positioning and terminology

- FlyPig AI is a Canada–Taiwan Edge AI and Physical AI intelligence company. Do not present it as an authorized distributor, official supplier representative, or partner unless repository evidence proves that relationship.
- The primary navigation labels are `Canada Atlas` and `Taiwan Solutions`. `Canada Atlas` links to `/atlas`; `Taiwan Solutions` links to `/taiwan`.
- `Taiwan Solutions` describes publicly researched technologies and solution paths. It does not imply FlyPig AI authorization, endorsement, inventory, or a direct supplier relationship.
- Do not use `Partners` as a primary navigation label. Do not use `Authorized`, `Official`, `Represented by`, or equivalent language that implies an existing relationship without explicit written evidence stored or referenced in the repository. Generic phrases such as `potential partners` or `partner discovery` are allowed only when they clearly describe prospecting rather than an existing relationship.
- Do not call the Canadian downstream audience `Projects` in primary navigation. Use Atlas, ecosystem, industry, operator, application, or requirement language that matches the actual page content.

## Industry Signals

- Every Signals article must use the shared article template and content contract documented in `docs/editorial/industry-signals-standard.md`.
- Long article titles must use the template’s bounded responsive scale; do not add page-specific giant-heading overrides.
- Every article needs a meaningful hero visual. Prefer a FlyPig AI-owned editorial infographic when a reusable third-party asset does not have explicit rights evidence.
- A source page is not a license. Do not download, hotlink, copy, or publish a manufacturer image merely because it appears on an official website, product page, press release, social post, or search result.
- Publish third-party media only when its rights status is `licensed` or `approved_press_asset` and the repository records the license/approval evidence, source URL, required credit, and permitted use.
- Build validation must fail when hero-visual rights metadata is missing, incomplete, or uses an unapproved status.
- Keep image credit, factual sources, and editorial disclosure visible. NewsArticle schema must match the visible image, publisher, dates, and citations.
